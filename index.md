## About Me


![Profile Picture]({{ "assets/images/SamKuzmishinHeadshot.jpg" | relative_url }}){: class="profile-image"}

 
My name is Sam Kuzmishin, and I am a Senior Mechanical Engineering student at Cornell University. I am interested in Aerospace and Automotive Engineering, as well as mechanical design. I am a member of Cornell Mars Rover, a student run enginering project team on campus, where I work to design, analyze, manufacture, and test our suspension before an annual competition in Utah. I also founded and operate Drive On, a nonprofit orginization dedicated to providing affordable and reliable transportation to the Pittsburgh community. This involves fundraising money to purchase car parts, and working with other local nonprofits to find individuals who need assistance.

Outside of my Engineering curriculum I am pursuing a Minor in Business and am a member of Cornell Consult Your Community, a group that provides pro-bono consulting services to small businesses in the Ithaca area. This taught me valuable skills, and culminated in me leading a team of analysts for a semester to help a local business create a market entry plan for a product. We did this by researching competitors, identifying clients, creating strategic partnerships, and producing financial tools for our client.
